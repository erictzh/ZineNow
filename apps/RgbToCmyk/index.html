What this is, and what it is not

  • This is a plain conversion: it moves the colours into CMYK with a simple formula. It does not use a printer's colour profile (ICC), so it cannot promise the colours a print shop's proof would show.
  • For black-and-white zines it does exactly what you want: everything ends up on the black plate only.
  • For colour, bright screen colours (neon, saturated blues and greens) cannot be printed in CMYK at all; they come out duller. Check a proof with your printer.
  • Left alone: pictures that are already CMYK or grey, use a colour profile or JPEG, patterns and gradients. The report above says how many were kept.
