# Remove background

**Remove background** keeps the main subject of a picture and makes everything else **see-through** (transparent), ready to place on another background. Two on-device models are offered, one for general subjects and one for portraits. You can also tune how tight the cut is, and optionally crop first.

- [U²-Netp and MODNet](ai-tools/remove-background/u2net-modnet): the two models and how a picture is prepared for them.
- [Cut-off and softness](ai-tools/remove-background/cutoff-softness): turning the model's answer into transparency.
- [Otsu](ai-tools/remove-background/otsu): an automatic threshold shown in the log.
