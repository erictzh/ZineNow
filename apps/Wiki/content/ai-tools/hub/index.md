# The hub

**Ai Tools (Local)** is one window with several small AI tools for pictures, **all running on your own device**: nothing is uploaded. The window is called the **hub** because the tools share everything around them: **one picture** in the middle, a **list of tools** on the left, an **options panel** and a **log** on the right, and a **Models page** where the AI files are downloaded or deleted.

This section explains those shared parts; the tools themselves follow in their own sections.

- [Shell and tool registry](ai-tools/hub/shell-registry): how the tools list and the window are put together.
- [Picture handling](ai-tools/hub/picture-handling): opening, zooming, undo, saving.
- [Log window](ai-tools/hub/log-window): the text log you can copy into a bug report.
- [Models page](ai-tools/hub/models-page): the list of AI models and their licences.
- [Dialogs](ai-tools/hub/dialogs): the "are you sure?" pop-ups before a download or a delete.
- [Send to ZEditor](ai-tools/hub/send-to-zeditor): passing a picture to and from ZEditor.
