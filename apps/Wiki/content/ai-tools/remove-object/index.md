# Remove object

**Remove object** erases something from a picture and fills the hole with what would plausibly have been behind it. You paint over the thing, press the button, and an AI model called **MI-GAN** (running on your device) invents the missing background.

- [MI-GAN](ai-tools/remove-object/migan): marking, the fill and how the result is pasted back.
- [Try another variations](ai-tools/remove-object/try-another): asking for a different answer.

> **Tip** The three non-AI fill methods (smooth, detailed, pattern) belong to ZEditor's Eraser and Marquee and are explained in [Remove and fill](zeditor/eraser-marquee/remove-and-fill). Ai Tools (Local) uses MI-GAN only.
