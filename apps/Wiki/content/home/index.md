# Home page

The **home page** (`index.html` at the top of the folder) is a small launcher: a card for each app, and a gear with a few settings. It reads its list of apps from `config/apps-config.js`, so adding an app is adding one entry.

- [App cards](home/app-cards): the cards, the About panel, and the "+" snippet generator.
- [Gear and the Experimental AI switch](home/gear-and-experimental): the settings gear and how AI features are switched on.
