## Intro

An honest list of what ZineNow does well, what is rough, and what is missing. It is made by one person, and the apps are young. If something here is wrong, or you find a problem that is not listed, please use **Report a problem** (home page or a gear panel).

## How to use

**Works, and used by its maker to make real zines:**
- **ZEditor:** text (frames, columns, text across pages, fonts you upload), pictures, shapes, pen, page layouts, guides, snapping, bleed, image effects including **halftone**, export to PDF, PNG, JPG and SVG (with outlined text, for Illustrator).
- **ZineArranger:** rearranging a PDF's pages onto printer sheets, and the minizine.
- **Markdown to PDF:** typeset books from Markdown.

**Newer, less tested:**
- **RGB to CMYK:** a *plain* conversion without printer colour profiles. Good for black-and-white zines; colour comes out duller than on screen.
- **Bleed and crop marks**, **coloured guides**, **text across pages** and **Outline Text to Shape**: built recently, checked in a desktop browser.

**Experimental (hidden until you turn it on):**
- **Ai Tools (Local):** AI that runs on your own device. It downloads models and needs a capable device.

**Not there:**
- **No Photoshop (PSD) files.** Use PNG, JPG or SVG.
- **No true CMYK colour management** (ICC profiles). See RGB to CMYK above.
- **Phones and tablets:** touch is supported, but the maker has tested mostly on a desktop browser. Expect rough edges and tell us.
- **No accounts, no cloud, no sync.** Save your project (`.znow`) yourself.

## How it works

ZineNow is free software and runs entirely in your browser; nothing you make is uploaded. The code is public (see **Source code** in the gear panels), so anyone can check these claims or build on it.
