## Intro

A **minizine** is a tiny booklet folded from **one sheet of paper** with **one cut**: eight small pages (front, back and six inside pages). You need a printer (or a print shop), scissors and about an hour. This is the quickest way to hold something you made in your hands, so it is the first thing to try.

## How to use

**1. Make the pages in ZEditor** (8 pages)
1. Open **ZEditor** and choose **New Document**.
2. Set **Paper size** to **Custom size (mm)** and type **74 × 105**. That is one eighth of an A4 sheet (called A7). On US Letter paper use **69 × 108**.
3. Set **Pages** to **8** and click **Create**.
4. Fill the pages: type with the **Text** tool, drop pictures in, draw with the pen. **Page → Layout** gives ready-made pages (picture and text). Keep your words about 5 mm away from every edge, because paper folds and printers need a margin.
5. Page 1 is the **front cover**, page 8 is the **back cover**.

**2. Send them to ZineArranger**
1. In ZEditor choose **Export → Send to ZineArranger**. It builds the PDF and opens ZineArranger with your pages loaded. (Or **Export PDF**, then open ZineArranger and use **Load PDF**.)
2. In ZineArranger set **Zine Size** to **Eighth (Minizine)**.
3. In the **Advanced** tab set the paper to **A4** (or US Letter). The preview shows one sheet with your eight pages in the right places, some upside down. That is correct: they come out the right way up after folding.

**3. Print**
1. Click **Print / Save PDF**.
2. In the print dialog choose your printer, the **same paper size**, **scale 100%**, **margins None**. Print **one side** only.

**4. Fold and cut**
1. Lay the sheet flat, printed side up. Fold it in half, then in half again, then in half once more, creasing each fold well. Open it fully: the creases split the sheet into **8 rectangles** (4 across, 2 down).
2. Fold the sheet in half **along the long middle crease** (the horizontal one), printed side **in** or **out** as ZineArranger's Folding Instructions say.
3. Cut **along that middle fold**, only across the **two middle rectangles**: start at the first vertical crease and stop at the third. Do not cut to the edges.
4. Open the sheet and fold it in half the **other way** (the vertical middle crease), so the cut opens into a diamond-shaped hole.
5. Push the two ends toward each other so the diamond closes into a cross shape, then fold the four pages round into a booklet and press it flat. Page 1 (the cover) ends up on the front.

ZineArranger also shows the **Folding Instructions** for the layout you chose. If anything here differs from them, trust the ones in the app.

## How it works

- Printed on one side, the sheet has a grid of **4 columns × 2 rows**. The top row of pages is **rotated 180°**, so after the fold every page is upright. This arrangement is called **imposition**: see [Imposition](zinearranger/imposition) and [Layouts](zinearranger/imposition/layouts).
- ZEditor builds the PDF; ZineArranger only **rearranges** its pages. Nothing is uploaded: both run in your browser.
- Printing is the browser's own: see [Print](zinearranger/print).

## Troubleshooting

- **The pages are in the wrong order.** Check page 1 is the cover and that Zine Size is *Eighth (Minizine)*; the preview shows the order.
- **The edge of the picture is cut off.** Printers cannot print to the very edge. Keep important things 5 mm inside, or print on a larger sheet and trim.
- **It prints too small or too big.** Set the print scale to **100%**, not "fit to page".
- **Something else.** Use **Report a problem** on the home page or in a gear panel.
